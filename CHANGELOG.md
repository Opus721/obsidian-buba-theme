# Changelog

What changed in each release of the Buba theme, in plain words. The newest release is at the top.

## Next (not released yet)

- Canvas cards have rounder corners and one quiet hairline instead of a shadow; a coloured card is a soft tint of its colour with one line in it.
- Pictures on a canvas show without a frame around them until you colour or select them.
- Group titles and arrow labels are small pills; a coloured group's title is written in its colour, and a dark custom colour is brightened so the title stays readable.
- Groups have a faint fill and rounder corners; a coloured group is a light tint with a thin line in its colour.
- The six canvas colours are Buba's bright row, and an arrow under the pointer or selected gets a little thicker.

## 1.1.3

- On a phone, the bookmark window keeps "Cancel" and "Save" as plain text buttons in its corners, in line with the fields and centred on the title, as in Obsidian; "Delete" is as wide as the fields.
- On phones and tablets, text fields are tall enough for a finger.
- In the Buba tree, the arrow after a long folder name stays in place, however many files the folder holds.
